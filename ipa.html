Advanced Form Cutter

Stress is optional — only applied when the part has $
Example: (') P L EI | Y I NG$

Use (y) for letters that drop → tid(y)|ies|ied|ying

(') = primary stress (ˈ)  ·  (ˌ) = secondary
$ = apply stress to this form only
* = full replacement (don’t append)

Form Cutter – Stress (important)

(') P L EI | Y I NG$

Result:

  • play → P L EI (no stress)
  • playing → ˈ P L EI Y I NG (stress applied because of $)

The stress marker (') or (ˌ) is only used on the forms that have $.
